Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 23, 2026

Game Corner (gamecorner.io) is a free website for playing party games with family and friends. One person signs in and creates a room, and everyone else joins with a link and a name. This policy explains what information we collect, why, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

If you create an account

  • Sign in with Google: Google shares your name, email address and profile picture with us. We only ask for this basic profile information (the openid, email and profile scopes). We never see your Google password and get no access to your Gmail, contacts, Drive or any other Google data.
  • Email and password: your email address and your password. Passwords are stored only as a secure hash by our sign-in provider; we cannot read them. We email you a one-time code to confirm the address is yours.
  • Account records: when your account was created and last signed in, and whether you can host rooms.

If you join a room as a guest

  • Guests don't need an account. We create a temporary anonymous ID so the game can tell players apart.
  • The name you type is shown to the other people in the room. It is not saved in our database.

While you play

  • Rooms: each room's code, which account hosts it and when it was created. Nothing else about the game is saved with it.
  • Game activity: moves, answers, votes, drawings and clues are sent live between the players' devices so the game works. They are not stored in our database. Secret information (such as your Werewolf role or the Impostor word) is encrypted so only you can read it.
  • Bunutan pictures: when you save or share your draw as a picture, it is created on your phone and goes only where you send it. The host just sees that you saved or shared it, not the name you drew.
  • Technical data: like any website, our hosting and sign-in providers record basic technical information such as IP address, browser type and request times, to keep the service running and secure.

Stored on your device

Game Corner saves a few settings in your browser's local storage: the name you last used, your last room, sound on/off, your game filters and the host's game in progress (so a refresh doesn't lose it), and your Bunutan draws (the name you drew, the budget and any note, so you can look at them again later). This stays on your device and you can clear it at any time in your browser settings. Our sign-in provider also stores a sign-in session there.

How we use your information

  • To sign you in, keep you signed in and confirm your email address.
  • To let you create and host rooms, and to show your name to the people you play with.
  • To keep the service working, secure and free from abuse.
  • To reply when you contact us.

We do not sell your information, show ads, use advertising or analytics trackers, or use your information for anything other than running Game Corner.

Google user data

Game Corner's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We use your Google name, email address and profile picture only to create and sign in to your Game Corner account. We do not transfer this data to others except the service providers listed below that run Game Corner, do not use it for advertising, and do not let humans read it except with your consent, for security reasons or to comply with the law.

Who we share it with

We use a few service providers to run Game Corner. They process data only on our behalf:

  • Supabase: accounts, sign-in, email codes, the rooms database and live game messages (servers in Singapore).
  • Vercel: hosts the website.
  • Google: only if you choose "Continue with Google".

We may also share information if the law requires it, or to protect the safety of our users or the service.

How long we keep it

  • Your account stays until you ask us to delete it.
  • A room is deleted when the host closes it, or when the host's account is deleted. We may also remove old rooms that are no longer used.
  • Game activity is never stored on our servers. Guest names disappear when you leave the room.

Your choices and rights

  • Delete your account: email us from the address on your account and we will delete your account and its data within 30 days.
  • Access or correct your data: email us and we will tell you what we hold and fix anything that's wrong.
  • Disconnect Google: you can remove Game Corner's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Clear local data: sign out, and clear this site's data in your browser settings.

Children

Game Corner is made for family game nights. Accounts are for adults and teens aged 13 or older (or the minimum age for online services in your country). Younger children can play as guests in a room hosted by a parent or another adult, without an account and without giving any personal information beyond a name. If you think a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

Security

All connections to Game Corner use HTTPS. Passwords are hashed, database access is restricted so people can only see their own data, and private game information is encrypted for the player it belongs to. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page. If the changes are significant, we will let account holders know.
